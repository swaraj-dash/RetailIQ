 ### Stage 1: The Shopper Perspective — Natural Language Discovery & Walkout Prevention

  • Who: Retail Consumer / Shopper
  • Screen: ShopperView.tsx

  #### ❌ The Real-World Problem:

  A customer walks into or searches a local store with an urgent need ("10,000 mAh USB-C power bank under ₹1,500 for travel, pickup today"). Their
  preferred model (VoltGo Pro RQ001) is out of stock. In 70% of physical retail visits, the shopper simply walks out empty-handed and buys from a quick-
  commerce competitor. This "walkout dilemma" costs retailers over ₹35,000 Crore annually.

  #### ⚙️ What RetailIQ Does:

  1. Natural Language Need Extraction (Gemini Operation 1): Gemini parses the messy query into structured constraints (Category: Electronics/Power, Max
  Price: ₹1,500, Battery: ≥10,000 mAh, Port: USB-C, Mode: PICKUP), while sanitizing against prompt-injection overrides.
  2. Fact-Grounded Substitute Matching: Identifies VoltGo Go (RQ002)—an in-stock alternative at Indiranagar Store (S1) for ₹1,299 (saving ₹200).
  3. Hardware Compatibility Verification (Gemini Operation 5): When the shopper clicks Compare Specifications, Gemini compares electrical protocols (20W
  PD vs. 22.5W SuperCharge) and verifies that VoltGo Go provides 90% peak charging speed and safely charges standard smartphones.
  4. Guaranteed 15-Minute Reservation Hold: The customer locks the unit with one click. The database executes an ACID row-lock (SELECT FOR UPDATE),
  decreasing Available-to-Promise stock from 10 to 9 with an active countdown timer.
  5. Order Conversion: The shopper converts the hold into a confirmed Store Pickup order.

  │ 💡 Problem Solved: A lost walkout is transformed into a guaranteed same-day sale in under 60 seconds with zero risk of the item being sold to someone
  │ else while the customer travels to the store.

  ### Stage 3: The Business Manager Perspective — Intelligent Supply Rebalancing

  • Who: Store Manager / Inventory Planner
  • Screen: BusinessManagerView.tsx (Decisions & Forecast Sub-tabs)

  #### ❌ The Real-World Problem:

  Because Indiranagar just sold down its inventory, it now faces an impending stockout on Day 2. Traditional retail software does one of two bad things:

  1. It does nothing until the shelf is completely empty (losing 2 days of sales).
  2. It triggers a Supplier Purchase Order, requiring a 36-unit Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) that ties up ₹25,380 of new cash, takes 7 days to arrive (5
  days too late), and accumulates excess inventory later.

  #### ⚙️ What RetailIQ Does:

  1. Dual-Model Forecasting & Backtesting: Runs 28-day Moving Average (MA28) vs. 7-day Seasonal Naive (SNAIVE7) to prove an impending stockout on Day 2.
  2. Network Rebalancing Engine: Scans sister branches and discovers that Koramangala (S2) has excess inventory (surplus supply).
  3. Transfer vs. Purchase Trade-off: Recommends transferring 32 units from S2 Koramangala to S1 Indiranagar instead of ordering from the supplier.
  4. Executive AI Explanation (Gemini Operation 3): Generates a crisp rationale explaining why transfer wins:
      • Arrival: 1 Day (arrives before stockout occurs).
      • Working Capital Required: ₹0 (uses existing network stock).
      • Supplier PO Avoided: Saves ₹25,380 capital lockup.
      • Captured Margin: +₹15,074.
      • Carbon Reduction: 42% lower freight emissions than a long-haul truck.
  5. Human-in-the-Loop Approval: The manager reviews the side-by-side trade-off matrix and clicks Approve Demo Transfer.

  │ 💡 Problem Solved: Prevents out-of-stock lost sales without spending a single rupee of new working capital.
