Problem Statement Alignment
The ₹35 Lakh Problem: Stockout Walkaways & Overbuying
Why traditional retail ERPs and naïve generative AI chatbots both fail in physical retail operations
02 / 10
1. The Shopper Dilemma Demand Side
  • 4.2% of retail sales are permanently lost when shoppers encounter an out-of-stock item on the shelf.
  • 72% of shoppers walk out of physical stores rather than manually searching for comparable specifications.
  • Severe journey friction: customers spend 45 minutes driving to a store only to discover phantom or missing inventory.
  • E-commerce monopolies capture this lost revenue with automated algorithmic substitute delivery.
2. The Reactive Merchandiser Supply Side
  • Merchandisers react to store stockouts by ordering high-MOQ supplier shipments (36+ units per SKU).
  • Suppliers require 14-day production lead times, during which customers continue to walk away empty-handed.
  • Over ₹35 Lakhs in working capital gets trapped in slow-moving warehouse pallets and holding fees.
  • Sister store Indiranagar (S1) runs out of stock while Whitefield (S2) sits on 32 units of dead excess stock!
3. The Generative AI Danger Tech Risk
  • Generative LLMs hallucinate phantom inventory balances and make up non-existent prices.
  • A single hallucinated inventory unit ruins store associate credibility and causes immediate fulfillment cancellations.
  • Naïve AI agents blindly buy inventory based on viral social media fads, leaving retailers bankrupt.
  • Enterprise retail operations require strict mathematical ledger guarantees and zero hallucinations.
Core Takeaway: Retailers do not need an AI chatbot that pretends to have stock; they need a deterministic closed loop.
