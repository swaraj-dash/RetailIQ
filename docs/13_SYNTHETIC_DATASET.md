# Deterministic synthetic dataset

Dataset `retailiq-demo-v1`; scenario date `2026-10-17`; currency INR. Every brand, product, cost, price, locality lane, customer session, sale and signal below is invented for demonstration. Locality names are real; stock and travel estimates are not. No retailer results or real product certifications are implied.

## Sales history (4,032 rows)

56 complete days × 24 SKUs × 3 stores. Let `j=1..24` SKU suffix, `s=1..3` store suffix, `k=0..55` offset from D-56. Default gross units=`(j+s+k)%3`; returned_units=0; in_stock_hours=24. Special overrides:

- RQ002/S1 all days3; S2 all days1; S3 all days1.
- RQ005 at all stores all days1.
- RQ006 at all stores units1 when `k%7=0`, otherwise0.
- RQ021/S3 units1 when `k%4=3`, otherwise0 (14 units in 56 days).
- RQ001 all stores prior sales default formula; today's zero stock is a newly observed stockout, not 56 days of lost demand.

Edge-case tests use separate fixtures with censoring/missing history, not mutations to canonical golden dataset. For live actions, add/increment daily sales at scenario date D. Current-day rows do not enter complete-day model fit.

## Internal and external signals

Create seeded sessions for each SKU/store: recent D-7..D-1 count40, prior D-14..D-8 count40, older D-35..D-15 count60. Spread count `n` uniformly across interval days. Each session has SEARCH and VIEW; a subset also CHECKOUT/PURCHASE, all same `session_key`, so deduplication is testable. Across the prior+older window, first 10 distinct sessions have purchase event (100 sessions total, c.10%). These represent a sampled online subset, not all-channel sales counts. Synthetic purchase events do not alter seeded stock or daily sales; they describe historical transactions already represented in aggregated sales.

RQ005/S1 override: recent60, prior20, older30, purchased5 of the50 prior+older sessions. RQ006 at every store override: recent8, prior7, older0, purchased0. RQ021/S3 has5 purchased sessions instead of10 so sampled purchases do not exceed its historical sales. Use explicitly SKU-linked events so category apportionment does not affect these golden counts. Duplicate events are prevented by unique keys; do not seed repeated click rows as extra sessions.
